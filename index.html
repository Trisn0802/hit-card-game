    How To Play

    Goal: Get a score closest to the target number.

    Rounds 1-6: Each round has 2 actions (player and bot).

    Hit: Place 1 card on the table, score increases.

    Stand: Do not place a card, score stays the same.

    Round 7: The system checks the winner based on score difference to target.

    Note: Maximum 5 cards on the table for each side.

    Health: Loser loses health based on how close the winner was to the target.

    Reshuffle: Replace cards in your hand with new ones (max 2 per game).

    Joker: Special card that can reshuffle opponent's hand or swap all cards.
